The tension

The first reply wins the booking.

A salon owner requests demos from two or three platforms on the same day. The team that replies while intent is warm gets the call. The team that replies on Monday gets the voicemail.

× THE WAIT

Follow up when there’s time

Demo requests land in a shared inbox and sit until someone gets to them. The hottest lead of the week cools into “just checking in” by the time it is answered.

✓ THE READ

Route at the speed of intent

Every request is scored the moment it arrives. The score decides who hears about it, and how fast, while the owner still remembers why they asked.

The rule: a demo request earns a score at arrival, and the score earns a response in minutes. After that it earns a reminder of what the wait costs.
The machine

Three moves, one workflow.

Sketched as an n8n workflow, built from the stack I actually run: ClickUp, n8n, Slack.

MOVE 01

Capture the intent moment

Every demo form submit lands timestamped in the CRM with the basics attached: business type, locations, current platform. The clock starts at zero, not at first human touch.

The buildWebhook to CRM, one record per request, deduplicated on domain and phone.
MOVE 02

Score the decay

A lead score that halves roughly every ten hours. No one has to feel anything about it. The number does the worrying, and the status pill flips from Hot to Warm to Cool on its own.

The buildScheduled run recomputes score and status from the request timestamp, no manual updates.
MOVE 03

Rescue the breach

When a Hot lead crosses the SLA without a booked demo, the workflow fires a Slack alert with full context and reroutes to the next available AE. Cold leads recycle into a re-engagement queue instead of rotting in the inbox.

The buildSLA timer, Slack message with lead context, escalation path logged back to the CRM.
