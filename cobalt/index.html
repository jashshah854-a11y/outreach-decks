The tension

Proof dies in the ticket.

A verified fix closes a ticket and the story ends there. Marketing never sees it, benchmarks never learn from it, and partners never hear about it.

Remediation proof→dies in closed tickets nobody rereads
Benchmark data→dies in PDF reports nobody queries
Partner wins→dies in silence, never told
The machine

One loop per leak. Agents do the routing.

Three n8n loops read what the platform already knows. Every artifact waits for a human before it leaves Cobalt.
