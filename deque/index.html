The tension

The community is the moat. Spray it and the moat drains.

Developer trust is why axe-core shows up everywhere. That trust is the top of the funnel, and mining the download list is the fastest way to lose it.

× THE SPRAY

Batch and blast the install base

Email every npm downloader. Score every GitHub star. The fastest known way to turn advocates into ex-advocates.

✓ THE READ

Let scan behavior raise its hand

Watch for account-level patterns in how teams scan. Reach out once, with something relevant to what they just did. Then stop.

The rule: a scan earns one timely, relevant conversation, or it earns silence. Nothing in between becomes a campaign.
The machine

Three signals. One conversation each.

Sketched as an n8n workflow: watch the signal, resolve it to an account, draft the one touch, hold it for a human.
