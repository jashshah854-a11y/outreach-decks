Two calendars. One decides the outcome.

Benefits outreach runs on a quarterly beat: open enrollment, newsletters, renewal reminders. A care episode runs in weeks. When the nudge follows the benefits calendar, it arrives after the decision is made.

The rule: outreach follows the care signal, not the campaign calendar. A referral issued is a sendable moment. A quarter is not.

Signals in, timed nudges out.

Sketched as an n8n workflow. Every stage earns its place by refusing to pass bad data along.

01

Ingest n8n schedule trigger

Eligibility and claims feeds land nightly, deduped into one record per member. Bad rows are quarantined with a reason, never silently dropped.

02

Listen webhook

Care signals watched in near real time: a referral is issued, a provider search runs, an appointment gets booked. Each one opens a window.

03

Verify compliance gate

Eligibility, consent and suppression lists checked before anything drafts. Stale or ineligible members never send, and every field carries a freshness timestamp.

04

Draft AI draft from the signal

The nudge is built from the signal itself: the member's plan, nearby Top Providers, and the deadline the window gives it. No generic blast copy.

05

Approve Slack approval

A human approves in Slack before anything sends. The machine finds the moment; a person owns the message.
