The leak

Rounds launch like campaigns. They die like them too.

Each sponsor round runs its arc, and when it ends, the relationship ends with it. The owners who almost won are the ones who never hear back.

LAUNCH

Applications flood the same path

A new round opens and owners come through profile, eligibility check, and application. The same windows, the same questions, every single round.

SELECT

Winners are announced, job done

Recipients are contacted by email and the round closes. As scoped, the work is complete.

SILENCE

Everyone else goes cold

Non-winning owners get nothing tailored to them. When the next round they qualify for opens, most never return, even though the platform already knows they are a fit.

The rule the current shape enforces: every round asks owners to start from zero, including the ones the platform already knows qualify.
The machine

The carryover engine.

A systems layer that keeps the relationship warm between rounds. Sketched the way I would build it: events in, eligibility out, one touch per owner.

STAGE 01

Eligibility ledger

Every application writes structured eligibility to the owner's profile: industry, stage, revenue band, owner demographics. The platform already learns this. The ledger just makes it reusable across rounds.

MovesProfile data stops being single-use. One application informs every future round.
STAGE 02

Round matching

When a new round opens, the engine scores past applicants against its criteria and flags who qualifies before launch. The bench is built before the announcement, not after.

MovesQualified reapplicants identified at round open, not discovered by luck.
STAGE 03

One-tap re-entry

Each qualified owner gets one touch: you are eligible, your details are ready, one tap to re-apply. Not a drip sequence. One moment, timed to the round.

MovesRe-application rate per round (sample metric, tracked per round).
For sponsors

Rounds stop starting at zero on their side too. Every round inherits a pre-qualified bench, so performance compounds round over round instead of resetting.

March: Amara applies→ Eligible, not selected→ June: new round opens→ One tap, re-applied

Amara applies to a grant round in March. She is eligible and not selected. In June, a new round opens with criteria she matches. The engine pre-fills her application from her profile, and she re-applies in one tap.

The March round never really ended for her. That is the whole idea.
