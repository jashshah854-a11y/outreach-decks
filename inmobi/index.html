The machine

One timeline. Four gates.

Sketched as an n8n workflow on top of ClickUp and Slack: check it, lock it, run it, follow it up.

LOOP 01

The checklist engine

Every vendor, badge, RSVP list, and slide deck gets a named owner and a due date in ClickUp. Anything stale gets pinged automatically, not discovered the night before.

In practiceAn n8n job flags every checklist item without a confirmed owner at T-7, and a Slack ping goes to the team channel.
LOOP 02

The run-of-show tracker

One live schedule for showtime: who is where, when, with what. Day-of changes go to one Slack channel, not five group chats.

In practiceA single ClickUp board, mirrored to Slack, so the booth lead always knows the next session.
LOOP 03

Lead capture to same-day routing

A badge scan is a lead the same minute, not a spreadsheet next week. Every scan routes to the right rep's queue with the conversation notes attached.

In practiceScans flow from the badge app into the queue, and the rep gets a Slack ping before the attendee leaves the booth.
LOOP 04

The T+1 follow-up

Every conversation gets its follow-up within one business day. Nothing sits in a spreadsheet waiting for someone to remember it.

In practiceA morning-after digest lists every lead, its owner, and whether the follow-up went out.
