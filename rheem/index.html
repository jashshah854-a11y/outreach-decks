The machine

Three layers. One memo.

Sketched as an n8n workflow: capture the signal at each layer, join it, write the one page that decides what happens next.

LAYER 01

The contractor layer

What the installers actually pushed. Training attendance, quote rates, install feedback from the field. This is where a launch lives or dies, and it is the layer most dashboards never see.

In practiceWeekly field capture joined to the launch calendar, so every product has a contractor verdict within the first month.
LAYER 02

The distributor layer

What moved through the channel. Sell-through by region, stockout flags, reorder velocity. The numbers exist; they just live in five spreadsheets.

In practiceDistributor data landed in one table every Monday, with the deltas highlighted before anyone asks.
LAYER 03

The homeowner layer

What the end customer chose. Search demand, review sentiment, rebate redemptions. Demand signals mapped back to the launch that caused them.

In practiceA monthly demand read that names which launch drove which spike, in plain language.
