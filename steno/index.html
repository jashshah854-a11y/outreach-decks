The gap

Field marketing is the relationship engine. The gap after it is where pipeline dies.

Attorneys buy from people they know, and your team is in the room at bar events, legal conferences, and networking gatherings across the country. The problem is what happens between "we were in the room" and "follow-up happened in 48 hours."

✓ THE 48 HOURS

The follow-up wins the firm

The engine

Three steps. All inside the 48-hour window.

Sketched as an n8n workflow on a lean stack: n8n, ClickUp, and Slack. No spreadsheet limbo.

STEP 01

Capture at the badge

Scans, QR codes, and roster imports land in ClickUp the same night the event ends, tagged by market and booth staffer.

The handoffEvery contact resolves to a territory and an owner before midnight, automatically.
STEP 02

Draft in context

n8n pulls the event, the conversation notes, and the attorney's practice area, then drafts the follow-up note for the person they met.

The touchOne personal note referencing the actual conversation. Never a drip sequence.
STEP 03

Human approves in Slack

The draft lands in a Slack channel for the booth staffer with a 48-hour countdown attached. Approve, edit, or hold.

The guardrailNothing sends without a human. The machine earns the timing; the person earns the trust.
