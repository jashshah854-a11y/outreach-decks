The machine

The funnel deserves the same engineering as the floor.

Three routing rules, borrowed from the contact center and aimed at the buying journey. Sketched as n8n workflows, with review checkpoints held in Slack.

RULE 01

Route by intent, not by round-robin

A demo request from an enterprise domain should skip the line the way a VIP caller skips the queue. Fit and behavior decide the route, not whose turn it is.

The n8n sketchWatch form and product signals. Resolve the account in ClickUp. Route to the right rep. Hold the summary in Slack for a human OK.
RULE 02

Hold the funnel to a service level

Every inbound gets a first response in minutes, measured and visible like any queue metric. A lead that waits a day is a caller who hung up.

The n8n sketchA timer starts on every new lead. Elapsed time posts to Slack. Breaches escalate like a missed SLA. The log lives in ClickUp.
RULE 03

Contain the tire-kickers, free the closers

Self-serve answers absorb the curious, the way a virtual agent absorbs the routine. Reps only take the conversations that need a human.

The n8n sketchQualify before a rep is paged. Instant, useful replies for the rest. Every outcome recorded in ClickUp for the next route.
The idea is simple: route the buyers coming to Talkdesk with the same care Talkdesk routes its callers. The machinery already exists. It just needs to point inward.
