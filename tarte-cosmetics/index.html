DTC is green. Staging PDP QA is done, inventory is synced, pricing is locked, and old URLs are swept. The home channel sets the reference every other lane matches.

The hold is the review queue, not the work. Listing copy and claims match the approved set, so approval is a waiting game. A watcher checks the listing twice a day and pings Slack the moment status flips.

Retail intake is signed off. Ship windows are met and no label has changed since signoff. If anything moves, the tracker flags it the same hour, not at the launch call.

Shade range mirrored with DTC. Every shade, price, and claim matches the home channel. Education kit is with the field team.

One open thread: the long-form cut is still in network review. Product, regulatory, and creative are cleared. This is the only lane that can move the date, which is exactly why the tracker exists: one red lane, seen by everyone, with an owner and a next check.

The tension

Drift is how launches fracture.

Five channels, five timelines, five definitions of ready. Without one tracker, each lane quietly invents its own.

TikTok Shop goes live a day early. QVC waits on a cut. Shoppers find mismatched shades and prices, and reviews land on the wrong listing.

✓ THE HOLD

One tracker, one date

Every thread reports to a single board. If a lane slips, the whole team sees it the same hour, and the date holds.

Launch day is not a date on a slide deck. It is five channels agreeing, at the same minute, that everything is ready.
The machine

What I would run from the coordinator seat.

Sketched on my real stack: ClickUp as the spine, n8n as the watcher, Slack as the alarm.

MOVE 01

One spine in ClickUp

Every launch lives as one task graph: product, regulatory, creative, and retail partner threads, each with an owner and a due date that counts back from launch day.

The payoffNothing is ready until every thread is ready, and everyone can see which thread is not.
MOVE 02

n8n watchers on the five doors

Scheduled checks sweep each channel listing for price, shade count, and live status. Any drift pings Slack before a customer sees it.

The payoffThe tracker updates itself between standups, so the morning check is reading, not hunting.
MOVE 03

A morning-of ritual

Launch week runs on a checklist, not memory: final QA pass, partner confirmation log, and one thread where every channel signs off.

The payoffThe launch call becomes a formality, because the board already said yes.
